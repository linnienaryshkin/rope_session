# Rope Session Consent Form

I'm a professional shibari/kinbaku specialist - provide commercial session to a regular people.

Kinbaku or Shibari sessions (just session further) - is a very diverse, complex embodiment practice, which include different type of rope work in a combination with other practices (or kinks)

Kink - we could think of that as a sexual thing, but it could be whatever... cooking for instance.

Here is my problem. I wanna perform a variety of different sessions, include rope work, connect with other kinks. And for that I'm doing an interview with person just before the session. But I couldn't make that interview for hours, usually it last 10-15 mins max... And than I start a session.

So basically, a session is: interview - play - after care - debriefing (usually next day)

What I would like is to have a page from a person about what they want to experience during the session, basically having all needed information from interview written down. And turn interview into simple confirmation.

We could use google forms, but there are three main problems with them:

- It's google, no one trust their data to the google...
- It's boring... filling forms before something so exited as kinbaku session... blah
- It's hard to adjust this form to a specific person... I, as a specialist, not ready myself to provide all variety of service to any person. my list is adjust by my feeling, how good i know the person, and simple mood today.
